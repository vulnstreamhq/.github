# Code of conduct

Vulnstream project spaces must be professional, respectful, and safe for
technical disagreement. This applies to repositories, issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and other project-managed communication.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, evidence, and impact rather than attacking people.
- Give and receive technical feedback directly and constructively.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Protect security reports and other sensitive information from unnecessary
  disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope while remaining free to
  disagree professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and
retaliation for good-faith reporting are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner through an existing
private channel. Do not expose a reporter, affected person, or sensitive
evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently exclude participants when necessary to protect people
and the project. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity,
pattern, impact, and the safety of affected participants.

Vulnstream is still a small private-alpha project. A monitored reporting route,
backup contact, conflict-of-interest process, and documented appeal path are
required before broader public community growth.
