# Code of conduct

Vulnstream project spaces must be professional, respectful, and safe for technical disagreement.

Communicate about ideas and evidence rather than people. Respect privacy and confidentiality. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect people, the project, and its users.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner while the community is small. A monitored independent reporting route is required before public community growth.
